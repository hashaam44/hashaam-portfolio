SkinAI Knowledge Retrieval

Prototype groundwork

Retrieving relevant passages from health-information documents to support responses.

Three WHO PDFs were processed into 446 text chunks, with 768-dimensional embeddings from nomic-embed-text stored in a ChromaDB collection named skin_disease_knowledge. Retrieval and response-safety modules were started. This is groundwork for the SkinAI assistant, not evidence that the complete system is production-ready.
